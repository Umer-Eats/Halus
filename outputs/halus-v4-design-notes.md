# Halus — revised image concepts
Image-only concepts made with the built-in image-generation tool. No website was built.

## Direction
Solid cranberry-red pixel Hal; approved rounded Halus wordmark and Rod of Asclepius mark. Red, medical white and silver in light mode; charcoal, white and softened red in dark mode. Empty architectural imagery, no generated people. Gen-X soft-club photographic haze, translucent controls and extended rounded type.

The new landing structure uses centered event discovery, a tabbed study preview, an event carousel and school-code entry. Dashboards use a top navigation dock and a main study or chapter workspace. School communities are private. Each student has one active annual event, per the requested Halus product model. Event cards are examples from the secondary division, not a complete catalog.

Rank labels follow the proposed Valorant-inspired ladder: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Ascendant, Immortal, Radiant. All names, scores and charts are illustrative. These generated images are visual explorations, not functional interfaces or finalized data/assessment specifications. Minor numerical and layout differences across variants need normalization in any later implementation.

## Research and design interpretation
- https://www.eightsleep.com/ — immersive full-width product presentation and staged product explanation informed the panoramic opening and progressive study tour.
- https://seed.com/ — guided discovery and horizontal product exploration informed the event finder and browseable event rail.
- https://21st.dev/@arunachalam/components/scroll-expansion-hero — media-led opening reference.
- https://21st.dev/@olewandowski1/components/features-4 — tabbed feature navigation reference; adapted to a single study preview.
- https://21st.dev/@designali-in/components/liquid-glass-button — tactile translucent control reference.
- https://hosa.org/guidelines — HOSA event/division reference.
Akoizo was used for the earlier pixel-companion reference, not as this revision's website-layout template.

These are design judgments drawn from the references, not claims that any source endorses Halus.
