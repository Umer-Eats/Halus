# Bloodstream animation model notes

The landing animation is a visual study of blood components. Red and white cells and platelets use approximate relative diameters; proteins are enlarged many times so their forms remain visible beside cells. It is not a scale diagram or an atomic model.

| Rendered form | Structural basis | Visual model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red blood cell | About 7.5 µm across, with a biconcave disc shape | Dimpled 3D membrane with thicker red rim |
| Neutrophil | About 12–15 µm across, with a nucleus of several connected lobes | Pale textured membrane, four connected nuclear lobes, chromatin shading, and fine cytoplasmic granules |
| Lymphocyte | Small forms are near red-cell size, with a large round nucleus and little surrounding cytoplasm | Rounded membrane, large central nucleus, and mottled chromatin |
| Monocyte | Roughly 12–20 µm across, with a folded or kidney-shaped nucleus | Larger, softly ruffled membrane, indented nucleus, and fine cytoplasmic texture |
| Platelet | Usually 1–3 µm across; resting platelets are discoid and contain granules | Flattened disc with granules, small surface pits, and a brighter marginal band |
| IgG antibody | Two heavy and two light chains form two Fab arms and an Fc stem; the Fc region carries glycans | Separate colored chain surfaces, paired domain bulges, Fab tips, and Fc glycan beads; enlarged for visibility |
| Insulin hormone | Two peptide chains joined by three disulfide links | Two differently colored folded strands, surface ridges, and three highlighted bridges; enlarged for visibility |

Sources: [NCBI peripheral blood smear](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK263/), [NCBI white blood cell histology](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK563148/), [NCBI monocyte histology](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK557618/), [NCBI platelet overview](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK53455/), [RCSB IgG structure](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1IGY), [RCSB insulin structure](https://pdb101.rcsb.org/learn/paper-models/insulin-activity-page).
